How to play

Click PLAY, then click the game viewport. Use WASD or the arrow keys to move and the mouse to look around. Left click fires, right click aims, R reloads and Space jumps.

The prototype runs locally with zero external runtime dependencies. Multiplayer networking is the next milestone.
